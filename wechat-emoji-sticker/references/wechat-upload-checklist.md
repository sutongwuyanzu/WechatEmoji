# WeChat Sticker Platform upload checklist

This checklist records requirements visible in screenshots supplied by the user. It is a point-in-time reference, not a live specification. Recheck the current upload page before preparing a real submission.

## Animated stickers

- GIF format; animation contains multiple frames and loops forever.
- Give each sticker its corresponding Chinese meaning label. The example set uses 开心、生气、委屈、大笑、疑问、好的、收到、没问题、嗯.
- Select a key frame as the sticker thumbnail in the upload interface.

## Album artwork

The sticker-album screenshots show these fields:

- Chat icon: PNG, 50 × 50 px; files over 100 KB are compressed by the platform. Prefer a simple, bright, uncluttered front-facing character head; avoid decorative extras, white outlines, jagged edges, and white backgrounds.
- Cover: PNG, 240 × 240 px; files over 500 KB are compressed. Use a recognizable half-body or full-body character, preferably facing forward. Set a transparent background; avoid white backgrounds, white outlines, and jagged edges. Keep the composition simple and avoid text except for text-only sticker packs.
- Detail-page banner: JPG or PNG, 750 × 400 px; files over 500 KB are compressed. Use an expressive scene related to the sticker set, with clear contrast against WeChat's interface. Avoid white/transparent backgrounds, distortion, and text.

## Artist profile artwork

A separate screenshot for the artist profile shows:

- Avatar: JPEG or PNG, 640 × 640 px, no more than 500 KB. A real-person avatar is recommended there.
- Personal homepage banner: JPEG or PNG, 750 × 400 px, no more than 80 KB.

Do not confuse the profile banner with an album's detail-page banner; their shown size limits differ.

## Appreciation art

The screenshots shared in this task show an appreciation guide image and a thank-you image field. Confirm the current formats, dimensions, and size limits in the active upload page; those details are not captured in the screenshots referenced here.

## Album metadata

The observed form has a name, short description, copyright field, category, style, and theme selections. Match labels and descriptions to the pack, confirm taxonomy in the live form, and do not invent claims of authorship or rights information.
